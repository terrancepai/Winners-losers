Sources and how the numbers are calculated

Where the data comes from

  • Fund and stock prices: Finnhub, every 15 minutes during market hours. Bitcoin: Coinbase. Treasury yields: FRED (daily, published with a one-day lag).
  • Top stories: picked from Finnhub's news feed once a day after the close and summarized by Claude in its own words. Each links to the original article.
  • Each asset class is priced with a real fund: .

Model portfolios (percent of portfolio)

  • Retirement Fund 2060 uses the underlying-fund weights in Vanguard's Target Retirement 2060 fact sheet dated September 30, 2026. Retirement Fund Income uses the fund's stated 30/70 stock/bond split. These portfolios mirror the funds' allocations; they are not the funds themselves.
  • The 5-day chart buys each portfolio at last week's close and holds it. Weekly and daily shocks use the same fund prices.

News shocks

    • News shocks isolate one effect. Bond moves use: price change ≈ −duration × yield change + ½ × convexity × (yield change)². Nvidia shocks use each fund's Nvidia weight (look-through).
    • Educational project, not investment advice.
