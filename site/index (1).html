Sources and how the numbers are calculated

Where the data comes from

  • Fund and stock prices: Finnhub, every 15 minutes during market hours. Bitcoin: Coinbase. Treasury yields: FRED (daily, published with a one-day lag).
  • Top stories: picked once an hour from Reuters, CNBC, Yahoo Finance and Bloomberg headlines and summarized by Claude in its own words from each headline and its short description. CNBC, Bloomberg and Yahoo Finance come from their public RSS feeds; Reuters comes through Finnhub's news feed. Each links to the original article; Bloomberg articles may need a subscription. If those can't be reached, other sources in Finnhub's feed are used.
  • Each asset class is priced with a real fund: .

Model portfolios (percent of portfolio)

  • Retirement Fund 2060 uses the underlying-fund weights in Vanguard's Target Retirement 2060 fact sheet dated September 30, 2026. Retirement Fund Income uses the fund's stated 30/70 stock/bond split. These portfolios mirror the funds' allocations; they are not the funds themselves.
  • The five-day chart buys each portfolio at the starting close and holds it. The week and each mover use the same fund prices.

How the biggest movers are chosen

  • For every company in the table below, the pipeline takes its five-day move × its weight inside each fund × that fund's share of the portfolio. Market forces are measured with real funds: interest rates with the bond funds, bitcoin, international stocks (VXUS), and "the rest of the U.S. market", which is what is left of each stock fund's move after taking out those companies.
  • Each portfolio shows the four that moved it most, gains or losses. Each mover shows only its own effect: everything else is held still.
  • Mover stories are written by Claude after each close, using only that company's headlines from Finnhub. Each links to its source.

What's inside the stock funds (percent of each fund)

  • S&P 500: SPDR S&P 500 holdings, Nov 9, 2026 (Micron, Tesla: Oct 2). Nasdaq-100: Invesco QQQ SEC filing, June 30, 2026 (Meta estimated). Total market: estimated at 0.85 × the S&P 500 weight. Weights drift with prices.
  • Bond sensitivity (DV01) uses typical durations: short Treasuries 1.9 years, U.S. bonds 6.0, international bonds 7.0 (half-sensitive to U.S. yields).
  • Educational project, not investment advice.
